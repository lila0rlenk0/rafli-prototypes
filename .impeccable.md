## Design Context

### Users

General consumers browsing and entering raffles/giveaways. Participants want to discover, trust, and enter raffles quickly. Hosts want to create and manage raffles with confidence. Used casually on phones and desktops — browsing, not grinding.

### Brand Personality

**Vibrant, trustworthy, approachable.** High energy without being chaotic. Premium feel without coldness. Playful accents grounded by strong typographic hierarchy and generous whitespace.

### Aesthetic Direction

- **Theme**: Light — warm cream base (`#f9f8f4`), dark charcoal text (`#141416`)
- **Accents**: Vibrant trio — cyan `#c4edff`, mint `#beffdb`, yellow `#f6ff8b` — used as large background blocks and badge fills, never as gradients on text
- **Typography**: Clash Display (semibold, 600) for all headlines/display. Geist Sans for body/UI. Strong size contrast between hierarchy levels
- **Signature moves**: `border-2 border-black` on interactive elements, pill-shaped badges, rotated "scattered stack" cards, character-by-character GSAP headline reveals, generous spacing (`max-w-[1720px]`, large section padding)
- **Radius**: Large and varied — `rounded-full` for badges/buttons, `rounded-3xl` for featured cards, `rounded-[30px]` for step cards, `rounded-[120px]` for hero decorative elements
- **Anti-references**: Crypto bro neon-on-dark, childish/cartoon, overly minimal/cold, generic SaaS dashboards

### Design Principles

1. **Warm contrast** — Vibrant accents on cream, not neon on dark. Energy comes from color blocks and typography, not glow effects
2. **Generous breathing room** — Large padding, spacious sections, premium feel through whitespace
3. **Strong affordance** — Black borders and solid fills make interactive elements unmistakably clickable
4. **Playful precision** — Rotated elements, scattered layouts, and animated reveals add personality within a structured grid
5. **Trust through clarity** — Clean hierarchy, readable type, honest UI — no dark patterns or visual noise
