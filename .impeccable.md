## Design Context

### Users

General consumers browsing and entering raffles/giveaways. Participants want to discover, trust, and enter raffles quickly. Hosts want to create and manage raffles with confidence. Used casually on phones and desktops — browsing, not grinding.

### Brand Personality

**Vibrant, trustworthy, approachable.** High energy without being chaotic. Premium feel without coldness. Playful accents grounded by strong typographic hierarchy and generous whitespace.

### Aesthetic Direction

- **Theme**: Light — warm cream base (`#f9f8f4`), stone near-black body text (`foreground` `#1c1917`), cooler near-black action surfaces (`primary` / `brand-dark` `#141416`). Body text and action fills use different near-blacks on purpose — `foreground` reads with the warm canvas, `brand-dark` pairs with the white hover flip. `primary` and `brand-dark` resolve to the same hex and are interchangeable.
- **Action colour**: Monochromatic — near-black `#141416` for every high-emphasis surface (buttons, badges, progress, links, selection). No cyan/blue action accent; the product leans on the hover flip + brand accent blocks for energy.
- **Brand accents**: Yellow `#f6ff8b` (countdown/landing only), mint `#beffdb` (success/participant/mobile-menu/on-chain verification), sky `#c4edff` (large landing blocks), green `#86ffae` (landing decorative shapes). Used as background blocks and badge fills, never as text gradients.
- **Typography**: Clash Display (semibold, 600) for all headlines/display — never below 18px. Geist Sans for body/UI. Strong size contrast between hierarchy levels.
- **Signature moves**: Button black↔white hover flip, `border-2 border-transparent hover:border-black` on cards, pill-shaped badges, rotated "scattered stack" cards, character-by-character GSAP headline reveals, generous spacing (`max-w-[1720px]`, large section padding).
- **Radius**: Large and varied — `rounded-full` for badges/buttons, `rounded-2xl` for raffle cards, `rounded-[30px]` for step cards, `rounded-[120px]` for landing decorative elements only.
- **Anti-references**: Crypto bro neon-on-dark, childish/cartoon, overly minimal/cold, generic SaaS dashboards.

### Design Principles

1. **Warm contrast** — Vibrant accents on cream, not neon on dark. Energy comes from color blocks and typography, not glow effects
2. **Generous breathing room** — Large padding, spacious sections, premium feel through whitespace
3. **Strong affordance** — Black borders and solid fills make interactive elements unmistakably clickable
4. **Playful precision** — Rotated elements, scattered layouts, and animated reveals add personality within a structured grid
5. **Trust through clarity** — Clean hierarchy, readable type, honest UI — no dark patterns or visual noise
