## Design Context

### Users

General consumers browsing and entering raffles/giveaways. Participants want to discover, trust, and enter raffles quickly. Hosts want to create and manage raffles with confidence. Used casually on phones and desktops — browsing, not grinding.

### Brand Personality

**Trustworthy, playful, modern, accessible.** Confident-casual tone — accessible enough that a first-time participant trusts the flow, authoritative enough that a host believes the outcome is provably fair. Copy direct ("Raffles! Done right!", "Your $1 could win!") — never hype, never crypto jargon.

### Aesthetic Direction

- **Canvas**: Warm cream `background` `#f9f8f4` — never pure white. White `#ffffff` reserved for card surfaces and text on black CTAs.
- **Action surface**: `rafli-black` `#141416` for every high-emphasis fill (CTAs, logo, headings, badge defaults, button fills). Cooler than the stone-family `foreground` `#1c1917` used for body text. The two near-blacks are NOT interchangeable — `foreground` reads on cream, `rafli-black` pairs with the white hover flip.
- **Interactive accent**: `primary` `#00b8ff` (cyan) — links, focus rings, selection states, progress fills, "Best value" badge. NOT the button color.
- **Brand accent trio (always used as a set)**: `rafli-yellow` `#f6ff8b` · `rafli-green` `#beffdb` · `rafli-blue` `#c4edff`. Decorative shape clusters, featured card backgrounds, marketing surfaces. Single use reserved per component (yellow = countdown, green = mobile nav, blue = trust section).
- **Typography**: Clash Display 600 for all display/headlines — never below 18px. Geist Sans for body/UI/buttons. Geist Mono for tx hashes and ticket codes only. Hero scales fluid `clamp(56px, 10vw, 120px)`.
- **Signature moves**: Black↔white button hover flip, `border border-transparent hover:border-black` on cards (1px, never 2px), pill-shaped buttons/badges, three-square pastel decorative clusters (24px radius, no borders/shadows/blur, flat fills only), character-by-character GSAP headline reveals, `1200px` container max-width, 80px section padding.
- **Radius scale**: `rounded-md` 8px (inputs), `rounded-lg` 12px (dialogs, role pills `8px`), `rounded-xl` 20px (raffle/featured/content cards, countdown timer), `rounded-full` (buttons, badges, pills, progress). Landing CTA buttons use one-off `10px`. Marketing-only `rounded-[120px]` blocks on landing — never inside app chrome.
- **Elevation**: Flat-first. Borders over shadows. 1px borders everywhere — no 2px anywhere. Restrained shadow ramp: `shadow-xs/sm/md/lg`. No glassmorphism, no backdrop blur, no heavy drop shadows in product UI. 3px focus ring at `ring/50` on `:focus-visible`.
- **Anti-references**: Crypto bro neon-on-dark, childish/cartoon, overly minimal/cold, generic SaaS dashboards.

### Design Principles

1. **Warm contrast** — Pastel accents on cream, not neon on dark. Energy from color blocks and typography, not glow effects
2. **Generous breathing room** — 80px section padding, `1200px` container, premium feel through whitespace
3. **Strong affordance** — Black fills and 1px black hover borders make interactive elements unmistakably clickable
4. **Playful precision** — Rotated decorative shape clusters and animated reveals add personality within a structured grid
5. **Trust through clarity** — Clean hierarchy, readable type, honest UI — no dark patterns, no hype copy, no crypto jargon
